Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

SupportPrivacy policy

This policy describes the free iPhone version of WHITEFALL and its support website. For questions or privacy requests, contact Hulio Studio at huliostudiosupport@gmail.com.

No ads, purchases or game accounts

WHITEFALL has no advertising, in-app purchases or subscriptions. We do not ask you to create a game account or provide your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or payment details to play. The game does not use advertising identifiers or cross-app advertising tracking.

Progress stored on your iPhone

Your records, earned unlocks, practice progress and settings are stored in the app's local storage. Eligible scores waiting for a Game Center connection may also be stored locally. These files are used to provide gameplay and preserve your progress; the game does not automatically upload them to a Hulio Studio server. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple backup settings.

Optional Apple Game Center

If you use Game Center, eligible ranked distances are sent to Apple's Game Center service for leaderboards. Scores may be displayed with your Game Center profile under Apple's settings and terms. Practice and continued runs are not submitted as ranked results. WHITEFALL can be played without Game Center or an internet connection.

Apple operates Game Center and controls its account information, retention and privacy settings. See Apple's Game Center privacy information. You can manage Game Center sign-in and profile settings in iPhone Settings. We cannot access your Apple Account credentials or delete Apple's account data on your behalf.

Diagnostics

The game and its Unity engine can produce local diagnostic information, such as startup timing and error logs, to help investigate reliability problems. WHITEFALL does not automatically send its local diagnostic files to our support inbox. Apple may separately provide crash or analytics information under your device's privacy and sharing settings and Apple's policies. If you choose to send us a log, screenshot or recording, we use it to investigate your request.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information or attachments you choose to include. We use these to respond, diagnose issues and follow up on your request. Messages are handled through our Gmail support inbox and may be processed by the email providers involved in delivery. We do not use support messages for advertising or sell them.

This website

These support pages do not contain advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies, embedded media or contact forms. When you visit a hosted website, its hosting provider may process normal network-request information to deliver and protect the pages. Email links open your email app; external Apple links are governed by Apple's policies.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local progress remains on your device until it is removed or app data is deleted; removing the app can erase your progress. Apple backup and Game Center data are managed through Apple's settings and services. We retain support correspondence while needed to resolve the request and maintain related support records. You can request access, correction or deletion of information you sent to support by emailing us. We will respond and delete information we control when no longer needed, except where retention is required to meet a legal obligation or resolve a dispute.

Children and sensitive information

We do not ask children to provide personal information to play. Please do not send sensitive personal information in support messages. If you believe a child has sent us personal information that should be removed, contact us so we can address it.

Changes and contact

If the game's data practices change, we will update this policy and its date. Questions and requests: huliostudiosupport@gmail.com.
