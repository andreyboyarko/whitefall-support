Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Game showcaseSupportPrivacy policy

Andrei Bayarka, operating under the name Hulio Studio, is responsible for the personal information handled by the developer as described in this policy.

This policy describes the free iPhone version of WHITEFALL and its game showcase and support website. For questions or privacy requests, contact Hulio Studio at huliostudiosupport@gmail.com.

No ads, purchases or game accounts

WHITEFALL has no advertising, in-app purchases or subscriptions. We do not ask you to create a game account or provide your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or payment details to play. The game does not use advertising identifiers or cross-app advertising tracking.

Progress stored on your iPhone

Your records, earned unlocks and settings are stored in the app's local storage. Best scores waiting for a Game Center connection may also be stored locally. These files are used to provide gameplay and preserve your progress; the game does not automatically upload them to a Hulio Studio server. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple backup settings.

Optional Apple Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your best completed distance is sent to Apple's Game Center service for each world's leaderboard. Scores may be displayed with your Game Center profile under Apple's settings and terms. Abandoned flights and legacy practice records are not submitted. Your place is requested when you tap MY WORLD RANK. Scores waiting for a connection are stored locally and retried when Game Center is available. WHITEFALL can be played without Game Center or an internet connection.

Apple operates Game Center and controls its account information, retention and privacy settings. See Apple's Game Center privacy information. You can manage Game Center sign-in and profile settings in iPhone Settings. We cannot access your Apple Account credentials or delete Apple's account data on your behalf.

Diagnostics

The release version does not create custom FPS, memory, thermal or startup performance journals and does not upload performance telemetry to Hulio Studio. The Unity engine or iOS may produce system error or crash information. Apple may provide crash or analytics information under your device's sharing settings and Apple's policies. If you choose to send a screenshot, recording or diagnostic report to support, we use it to investigate your request.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information or attachments you choose to include. We use these to respond, diagnose issues and follow up on your request. Messages are handled through our Gmail support inbox and may be processed by the email providers involved in delivery. We do not use support messages for advertising or sell them.

This website

This game showcase and its support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. The showcase contains self-hosted gameplay images and a video that loads when you play it. The site has no developer-added advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies or contact forms. Its language switch does not store a preference. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, under its privacy statement. Email links open your email app; external Apple links are governed by Apple's policies.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local progress remains on your device until it is removed or app data is deleted; removing the app can erase your progress. Apple backup and Game Center data are managed through Apple's settings and services. We retain support correspondence while needed to resolve the request and maintain related support records. You can request access, correction or deletion of information you sent to support by emailing us. We will respond and delete information we control when no longer needed, except where retention is required to meet a legal obligation or resolve a dispute.

Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy of information you provided, and complain to your local data protection authority. We use support correspondence to respond to your request and for our legitimate interest in resolving technical problems. Service providers may process information outside your country under their applicable privacy terms and safeguards. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Children and sensitive information

We do not ask children to provide personal information to play. Please do not send sensitive personal information in support messages. If you believe a child has sent us personal information that should be removed, contact us so we can address it.

Changes and contact

If the game's data practices change, we will update this policy and its date. Questions and requests: huliostudiosupport@gmail.com.
